About SMARTFLOOD

SMARTFLOOD is an ESP32-based water-level monitoring and warning system. A water sensor is read by an ESP32, which classifies the level as NORMAL, WARNING or DANGER, lights a green, yellow or red LED (with a buzzer for DANGER) and sends the reading over Wi-Fi to this Django dashboard.

Status meaning

  • NORMAL Water is below the warning threshold.
  • WARNING Water has reached the warning threshold.
  • DANGER Water has reached the danger threshold.

Important note

The sensor value is a raw electrical reading (0–4095), not a depth in centimeters. Converting it to centimeters needs calibration with your own sensor and container. Low-cost water sensors also corrode over time, so this system is for learning and early warning only, not a certified flood-safety device.
